Tic-Tac-Nope variants
====================

All variants use alternating O/X turns and public mystery-cell locations.
Each player may attempt each mystery cell once. A mystery attempt claims the
cell only when it is unoccupied, always consumes the turn, and does not reveal
success or failure to the acting player. The opponent sees only that a mystery
action occurred.

standard - Hidden Tiles
-----------------------
The nine-cell board uses ordinary three-in-a-row winning lines. A mystery cell
may be attempted on the first move. A full board without a winner is a draw.

no-hidden-opening - No Hidden Opening Move
-------------------------------------------
This is the standard game except the first move must be a visible cell. Hidden
cells become legal from the second move. At least one visible cell is required.

no-center-ring - No Center
--------------------------
Cell 5 does not exist and cannot be occupied or hidden. The playable cells form
the circular order 4, 1, 2, 3, 6, 9, 8, 7, then back to 4. Any three
consecutive cells in this cycle win, including 4-1-2 and 7-4-1. Hidden cells
may be attempted on the first move. A full perimeter without a winning triple
is a draw.

no-center-ring-pair-loss - No Center, Pairs Lose
------------------------------------------------
This uses the same eight-cell perimeter and hidden-attempt rules as no-center.
Three-in-a-row has no special meaning. Two cells are adjacent when they are
consecutive in the circular perimeter, including 7-4 and 4-1. The first
player whose successful move creates an adjacent pair immediately loses. A
failed mystery attempt cannot create a pair. A full perimeter without a pair
is a draw.
