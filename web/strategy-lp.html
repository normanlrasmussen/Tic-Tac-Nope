EXACT EQUILIBRIUM FORMULATION

Exact Nash (Sequence-Form LP)

Solve the actual imperfect-information zero-sum game globally instead of learning toward equilibrium from sampled regret.

Sequence form compresses mixed strategies over enormous complete contingency plans into realization weights on action sequences. For finite two-player zero-sum games with perfect recall, the resulting minimax problem is a linear program.

O'S SEQUENCE-FORM LP
max fᵀp   s.t.   Ex=e, x≥0, Fᵀp≤Aᵀx

FROM MIXED TO BEHAVIORAL

What the LP actually solves

REALIZATION PLAN

x and y assign probability mass to player action sequences. Flow constraints ensure the child sequence masses at an information set sum to the parent sequence mass.

PAYOFF MATRIX

A is sparse. Each terminal history contributes its O utility to the entry indexed by O's terminal sequence and X's terminal sequence.

BEHAVIORAL POLICY

At a reached information set, recover local play with σ(a|I)=x(σ(I)a)/x(σ(I)). Perfect recall makes this realization-equivalent to the corresponding mixed strategy.

WHAT IT OPTIMIZES

The global minimax value

O maximizes the payoff it can guarantee against every legal X strategy; X symmetrically minimizes O's payoff. The two solved LPs provide lower and upper bounds on the same game value.

v = maxₓ minᵧ xᵀAy = minᵧ maxₓ xᵀAy

WHY SEQUENCE FORM

Avoid the normal-form explosion

Normal form would need one variable per complete pure contingency plan. Sequence form instead scales with information sets and action sequences, which is exponentially smaller in many extensive-form games while remaining exact.

THEORETICAL STATUS

What can you actually claim?

GUARANTEE

If the complete unabstracted Tic-Tac-Nope tree is encoded correctly and the LPs solve to optimality, the returned realization plans are a Nash/minimax equilibrium of the implemented finite two-player zero-sum perfect-recall game, up to numerical solver tolerance. Strong duality gives a direct value/optimality certificate.

DOES NOT GUARANTEE

A floating-point solve is not symbolic exact arithmetic. Nash need not be unique, need not be a sequential-equilibrium refinement, and need not maximize exploitation of a particular weak opponent. Any abstraction or pruning that changes the full game also changes the guarantee.

COMPUTATIONAL PROFILE

Why it is offline

The full game still contains millions of histories for ordinary mystery-cell configurations. Sequence form removes the pure-plan explosion but still requires rows and columns for the game's information structure. Building that sparse LP on a mobile browser is not an appropriate live-game workload.

CERTIFICATE

What an exported solve records

The repository solver exports both players' behavioral policies, the O value lower and upper bounds, the LP duality gap, game-tree counts, and solver status. The site should only label a loaded policy exact when that certificate accompanies the full solve.

LP VS. MCCFR

Same equilibrium target, different computational route

MCCFR is the practical live strategy: it estimates counterfactual regret from sampled trajectories and approaches the Nash set asymptotically. Sequence-form LP is the exact benchmark: it solves the global minimax optimization directly and can expose an explicit duality-gap certificate. Comparing MCCFR exploitability to the LP solution is the cleanest way to demonstrate convergence.

BOTTOM LINE

The strongest equilibrium statement on the site—conditional on completing the full LP solve.

The implementation deliberately does not pretend that a finite MCCFR table is an LP solution. Until a full sequence-form artifact is generated for a configuration, this strategy remains an offline benchmark rather than a selectable live opponent.
